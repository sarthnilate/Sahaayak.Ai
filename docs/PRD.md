# Sahaayak AI --- Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Summary
Sahaayak AI ("From Chaos to Action") is an AI-assisted community incident-intelligence platform designed to aggregate fragmented community reports during urban crises (focusing on simulated heavy rainfall, localized waterlogging, and road blockages). It extracts structured facts, clusters duplicates with explainable rationale, surfaces corroborating and conflicting evidence, calculates an explainable priority level, and presents actionable follow-up tasks to authorized coordinators for human approval and tracking.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Explainable AI Integration**: Powered by Google Gemini (via official Google GenAI SDK) with structured outputs, plus a deterministic offline demo adapter.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards**: AI recommends; human coordinators confirm report associations and approve emergency tasks. No autonomous dispatch of real-world emergency services.
3. **Multi-lingual Community Intake**: Native support for English, Hindi (हिंदी), and Marathi (मराठी) text inputs and simulated field photos.
4. **Evidence & Truth Grounding**: Clear tagging of claims as Corroborated, Conflicting, or Unverified. No fabricated sources or coordinates.
5. **Operational Resiliency**: Robust audit logging, persistent storage (survives restarts), and graceful degradation if AI or tile services are unavailable.

## 3. User Personas
- **Field Reporter / Citizen**: Submits ground reports (text, descriptions, landmark references, images) across languages.
- **Incident Coordinator / Dispatcher**: Reviews structured extractions, verifies evidence, confirms/rejects duplicate links, overrides priorities with justification, and assigns tasks.
- **Auditor / Administrator**: Inspects the immutable audit history and reviews system integrity.

## 4. Key Workflows
1. **Report Submission**: Intake of text descriptions and photos; validation of file size and MIME types.
2. **Information Extraction**: Normalization into structured fields (hazard type, severity, location claim, water depth, affected infrastructure, urgent needs) using Google Gemini or Demo adapter.
3. **Duplicate Detection & Incident Linking**: Multi-signal matching (category, location proximity, temporal closeness, text similarity) presenting candidate links for coordinator approval.
4. **Incident & Evidence Management**: Grounded mapping, evidence verification state, conflict flags, and timeline.
5. **Task Lifecycle**: Action recommendations generated by rule-based and AI analysis; approved and tracked through `pending -> in_progress -> completed`.
6. **Audit Trail**: Every consequential coordinator decision and status change logged with timestamp and user ID.
